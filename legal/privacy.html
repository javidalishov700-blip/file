Privacy Policy

PDF Studio · Developer: Javid Alishov · Last updated 16 October 2026

Your documents never leave your iPhone. Every scan, conversion, merge, edit, signature and OCR in PDF Studio runs on your device — nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else. The one thing that uses the network is advertising: the app shows ads served by Google AdMob, described below.

The short version. We have no server and no account system, and we never see your files. Google AdMob, which serves the ads, collects device and usage data under its own privacy policy — see "Advertising".

Your documents

  • Files you create are stored in the app's own folder on your device ("My Files"), which is also visible in Apple's Files app under On My iPhone › PDF Studio.
  • Files you pick for a tool are copied into a temporary folder on your device only for as long as the tool needs them.
  • Text recognition (OCR) uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Office and web page conversion uses Apple's on-device WebKit.
  • Your last drawn signature is kept privately on your device so you can reuse it. Delete the app to remove it.
  • Deleting a file in My Files, or deleting the app, deletes your documents. We have no copy.

Permissions

  • Camera — only when you choose "Scan with camera", to capture the pages you scan. Images stay on your device.
  • Photos — the system photo picker gives the app only the photos you select; the app cannot see the rest of your library.
  • Tracking — Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, for advertising (below).

Network access

Apart from loading ads, the app only connects to the internet when you use HTML to PDF: it then loads the web address you typed, directly from that website, just as a browser would.

Advertising

PDF Studio is free and shows ads (a banner and an occasional full-screen ad) served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and other device identifiers
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown, viewed or tapped
  • General device and app information (device model, OS version, IP-derived approximate region, language) used for ad delivery and fraud prevention

On first launch iOS shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown, just less personalised. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are also asked for consent through Google's consent message; you can change that choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us — we never see it. See Google's privacy policy, how Google uses data from partner apps and Google Ad Settings.

Children

PDF Studio is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we hold none. The only third-party code in the app is the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's consent SDK. Apple distributes the app under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights over personal data a company holds about you. We hold none; your files are on your device and under your control. For data AdMob collects, use Google's controls above and your device's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app stores or sends, this page will be updated before that version ships and the date above will change.
