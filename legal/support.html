PDF Studio · Developer: Javid Alishov

PDF Studio scans, converts, merges, splits, compresses, edits, signs and protects PDFs — all on your iPhone or iPad.

Fastest way to reach us. Email javidalishov700@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version and the tool you were using.

Where are my files?

In the My Files tab, and in Apple's Files app under On My iPhone › PDF Studio. Use the share button to send a file anywhere.

Do my documents get uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

My scanned PDF isn't searchable

Turn on "Make text searchable (OCR)" when scanning, or run the OCR PDF tool on an existing scan.

Compression didn't make my file smaller

Text-only PDFs are already small; the app keeps the original rather than making it bigger. Compression works best on scanned or photo-heavy PDFs. Compressed pages become images, so their text is no longer selectable.

PDF to Word doesn't look exactly like the original

The text and paragraphs are converted into an editable document; complex layouts such as tables and columns are simplified.

I forgot the password of a protected PDF

Passwords can't be recovered — Unlock PDF only works when you know the password.

Are there ads?

Yes, the app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. You can change your ad consent in Settings › Ad privacy choices (EEA/UK) and in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Requirements

iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. Camera scanning needs a device with a camera.
