## English

**Promotional text (170)**
Scan, convert, merge, compress, edit and sign PDFs — all processed privately on your iPhone. Your documents are never uploaded.

**Keywords (100)**
pdf,scanner,converter,merge,compress,editor,sign,ocr,word,jpg,split,watermark,document,scan,docx

**Description**
PDF Studio is an all-in-one PDF toolkit that works right on your iPhone and iPad. Every tool runs on your device — your documents are never uploaded to a server.

SCAN
• Scan paper documents with the camera — automatic edge detection
• Color, grayscale or black & white
• OCR: make scans searchable and selectable

ORGANIZE
• Merge PDFs in any order
• Split by page, by ranges, or extract pages
• Reorder, rotate and delete pages
• Rotate whole documents

OPTIMIZE
• Compress PDFs (3 levels)
• Crop page margins

CONVERT
• Photos / JPG to PDF
• Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, RTF and text to PDF
• Web page (URL) to PDF
• PDF to JPG / PNG
• PDF to Word (DOCX)
• PDF to text or Markdown

EDIT & SIGN
• Add text, images and freehand drawings
• Highlight with the marker
• Draw your signature once, reuse it anywhere
• Watermarks and page numbers

SECURITY
• Protect PDFs with a password
• Remove a password you know

All your files are kept in My Files and in the Files app. PDF Studio is free and supported by ads.

PDF Studio, iPhone ve iPad'inde çalışan hepsi bir arada PDF uygulamasıdır. Tüm araçlar cihazında çalışır — belgelerin hiçbir sunucuya yüklenmez.
